Household registration statistics data analysis in Dec 2025

Published Date: 2026-01-09
1. According to the household registration data, there were 9,851,532 households at the end of Dec 2025. Compared with the same month last year, there increased 364,052 households with an increase rate of 3.84%.
2. By the end of Dec 2025, the total population was 23,299,132 with a decrease of 101,088 compared with the same month last year.
3. By the end of Dec 2025, the male population was 11,462,401 and female population was 11,836,731. The sex ratio was 96.84 compared with male to female.
4. There were 9,027 babies born in Dec 2025 which decreased 27.76% compared with the same month last year. The average showed that a baby was born about every 4.9 minutes. The annual crude birth rate was 4.56‰.
5. There were 17,450 deaths in Dec 2025 which increased 0.03% compared with the same month last year. The average showed that there was about one death at every 2.6 minutes. The annual crude death rate was 8.82‰.
6. The couples of marriage were 10,816 in Dec 2025. Including 10,456 couples of different gender and 360 couples of the same gender. The annual crude marriage rate was 5.47‰.
7. The couples of divorce were 4,674 in Dec 2025. Including 4,576 couples of different gender and 98 couples of the same gender. The annual crude divorce rate was 2.36‰.
8. There were 74,682 in-migrants in Dec 2025 which decreased 13.68% compared with the same month last year. In the same time there were 73,212 out-migrants which decreased 12.99% compared with the same month last year.
9. At the end of Dec 2025, the indigenous people was 629,456 with an increase of 17,782 compared with the same month last year (2.70% of the total population).
10. At the end of Dec 2025, the population aged 0 to 14 was 2,681,890 (11.51% of the total population). The population aged 15 to 64 was 15,944,087 (68.43%). The population aged over 65 was 4,673,155 (20.06%). In addition, the population aged over 20 was 19,644,140 (84.31%).
